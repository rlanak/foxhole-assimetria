# Artillery and field weapons

**Bottom line:** balanced, with different philosophies. Colonials have more mobility and bigger calibres on field guns. Wardens have longer-range emplaced artillery and more 250mm variants.

| Role | Colonial | Warden | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large field gun | 945g "Stygian Bolt": 5,000 HP, 94.5mm | Balfour Stockade: 5,000 HP, 75mm | Colonial (bigger calibre) |
| Heavy field gun | 40-45 "Smelter": 4,000 HP, 40mm | Balfour Rampart: 4,000 HP, 68mm | Warden (bigger calibre) |
| 250mm field mortar | Tisiphone: 2,450 HP + Alekto (large version, 3,750 HP) | Balfour Falconer: 2,850 HP | Colonials have the large version |
| 250mm on a tank | HC-7 Ballista | Silverhand Chieftain (with 12.7mm MG) | Equivalent |
| 120mm light artillery | 120-68 "Koronides": mobile, 100–250 m, 9.2 rpm | Huber Lariat (emplaced): 100–300 m, 8 rpm | Colonial mobile, Warden longer range |
| 150mm artillery | 50-500 "Thunderbolt": 200–350 m, 7.1 rpm | Huber Exalt (emplaced): 100–300 m, 8 rpm | Colonial reaches further |
| Mobile incendiary rockets | T13 "Deioneus": 350–400 m | Niska-Rycker Skycaller: 275–350 m | Colonial (+50 m) |
| HE rockets | R-17 "Retiarius": 375–500 m; Hades' Net (emplaced): up to 575 m | O'Brien V.200 Squire: 375–500 m | Mirrored, Colonial has the longest emplaced option |
| 150mm battle tank | Lance-46 "Sarissa" | Flood Mk. IX Stain | Same stats and cost |

Field weapon data in [`data/field-guns.csv`](../data/field-guns.csv).

> The Storm Cannon (300mm, up to 1,000 m) and the Tempest Cannon RA-2 (300mm on rails) have no faction listed on the wiki. The faction of the Huber and Thunderbolt pieces follows naming convention, as the pages consulted don't state it.
