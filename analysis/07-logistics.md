# Logistics and backline

**Bottom line:** for backline players the factions are practically identical. Trucks and most logistics vehicles have the same stats and only differ in model. Facilities, resources and the large logistics ships (Longhook, Bluefin, Bowhead etc.) have no faction.

- **Top-tier vehicles cost exactly the same.** Lance and Flood, Sarissa and Stain, Ares and Predator, Titan and Callahan all cost the same. The difference is what each side gets for that price, and the Predator is the clearest case.
- **Entry-level vehicles are slightly cheaper for Colonials.** The Xiphos costs 25 Refined Materials versus 40 for the Knave, and the Hatchet 115 versus 120 for the Devitt.
- **Recent changes are symmetric.** Resource Transfer Station capacity doubled from 15,000 to 30,000 in Update 66. The Lunaire F cost increase (15 to 35 Refined Materials) in Update 67 pulls more Refined Materials from Colonial logistics.
- **Map.** Community reports point to better roads on the Colonial side and poor Warden water access in some regions, which favours Colonial logistics regardless of equipment.

What weighs most on the backline isn't equipment asymmetry: it's population and time zones, which decide who can protect facilities outside peak hours.
