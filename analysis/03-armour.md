# Vehicles and armour

**Bottom line:** the Warden advantage is clear in one place: the super tank. Elsewhere, Colonials have more HP in almost every class, while Wardens have more speed, more specialised types and more anti-infantry weapons. The current pattern is **Colonial = more HP, Warden = more speed and variety**.

## Lance-36 vs Flood Mk. I

They're closer than their reputations suggest. Same cost (50 Steel, 30 AM III, 60 AM IV, 35 AM V), same armour (15,650) and same 75mm gun (1,750 damage, 6 s reload, 35 m). The Lance has 1,150 more HP and a hull MG that does almost double the damage (66–98 vs 35–52). The Flood is 15% faster (7.22 vs 6.25 m/s on road), which matters for disengaging and repairing.

## Ares vs Predator

This is the biggest armour imbalance in the game. At the same cost (275 Steel, 3 Rare Alloys etc.), the Predator has 30% more HP, 2,000 more armour and is 44% faster. It also carries two 360° quad grenade launchers. The Ares has the double-barrel advantage (3,500 damage per volley vs 2,187 for the Predator), but the wiki itself says it is slower and worse at escaping.

## Anti-infantry weapons

On the Colonial side, the Bardiche (12.7mm coaxial MG), Ranseur (12.7mm), Nemesis (quad grenade launcher), Scorpion (twin 12.7mm), Vulcan (flamethrower) and Lance-36 all carry anti-infantry weapons. Outside the tank list there are also the Actaeon and Vesta tankettes.

Even so, the Warden trend is real: 10 of 19 Warden tanks carry an MG, flamethrower or grenade launcher, versus 6 of 17 Colonial tanks. The whole Gallagher line has MGs. The gap is widest at the top: the Flood Juggernaut has a flamethrower and the Predator two grenade launchers, while the Ares has no anti-infantry weapon at all.

## Update 68 dev branch (preview, not live)

Tank engineer seats can switch between modes: forward (higher top speed, lower reverse speed), reverse (the opposite), turn (tighter turning radius), targeting (faster turret rotation) and overheat (lessens the effect of being tracked before the tank is immobilised). The mode system applies to both factions, and the patch notes give no numbers. If the size of the speed bonus doesn't depend on faction, the Flood's 15% road speed lead over the Lance-36 stays the same in relative terms.
