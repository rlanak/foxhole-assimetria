# Infantry

**Bottom line:** a technical draw, with a slight Colonial edge on the core weapons. The basic Colonial rifle fires almost 50% faster for the same damage. Warden advantages are the long rifle and the SMG magazine.

| Class | Colonial | Warden | Edge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic rifle | Argenti r.II: 45–67 dmg, 81 rpm, 12 rounds | No.2 Loughcaster: 45–67 dmg, 55 rpm, 12 rounds | Colonial (+47% rate of fire) |
| Auto rifle | Catena rt.IV: 45–67, 133 rpm, 10 rounds | Sampo 77: 36–54 auto at 240 rpm or 45–67 semi at 72 rpm, 12 rounds | Depends on use |
| Burst rifle | Fuscina pi.I: 39–58, 180 rpm burst, 18 rounds | Blakerow 871: 39–58, 112 rpm, 15 rounds | Colonial |
| Heavy rifle | Volta r.I: 78–117, 46 rpm, 8 rounds, 29.5–35.5 m | Hangman 757: 78–117, 56 rpm, 6 rounds, 22.5–32.5 m | Colonial on range, Warden on rate of fire |
| Long rifle | KRR2-790 Omen: 30 rpm, 8 rounds, 35–45.5 m | Clancy Cinder M3: 42 rpm, 12 rounds, 33–43 m | Warden |
| Sniper | KRR3-792 Auger: 10 rounds, 54–59 m | Clancy-Raca M4: 5 rounds, 57.5–62.5 m | Even (magazine vs range) |
| Assault rifle | "Dusk" ce.III: 300 rpm, 40 rounds | Aalto Storm Rifle 24: 240 rpm, 25 rounds | Colonial |
| SMG | "Lionclaw" mc.VIII: 240 rpm, 20 rounds | Fiddler 868: 240 rpm, 25 rounds | Warden (bigger magazine) |
| Shotgun | KRF1-750 Dragonfly: 70–105, 5 shells, 17–25 m | No.4 Pillory: 63–94, 2 shells, 120 rpm | Depends on use |

Full data in [`data/small-arms.csv`](../data/small-arms.csv).

> The wiki doesn't label the faction of each small-arms table. The split above follows the naming convention (Latin and Greek for Colonial, English and Gaelic for Warden). Corrections are welcome.

## Anti-tank and grenades

- Warden: Bonesaw (ARC/RPG, 647 armour-piercing damage, 37 m) and Cutler Launcher 4 (RPG, 524 explosive, 32 m).
- In Update 67 the KLG901-2 Lunaire F was nerfed: reload from 2 s to 3 s and cost from 15 to 35 Refined Materials, to bring it closer to the Cutler.
- Community report: the Tremola grenade was buffed and made Colonial-only, which unbalanced the economy between the two factions' grenade launchers.
