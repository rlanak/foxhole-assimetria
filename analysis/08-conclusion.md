# Conclusion

**Overall the game is reasonably balanced, but there are concrete, measurable imbalances**, concentrated in the super tank, fighters and air strikes on ships. The idea that "Wardens have everything better" doesn't hold up against the numbers.

| Common claim | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wardens are better at infantry | Not supported | Colonial basic, burst and assault rifles are better; Wardens win on long rifle and SMG |
| Wardens are better at armour | Partly | Predator > Ares by a wide margin; elsewhere Colonials have more HP and Wardens more speed, variety and anti-infantry weapons |
| Wardens are better at naval | Not supported | Colonials have more HP in 5 of 9 classes at the same cost; the Nakki is the better ambush submarine |
| Wardens are better in the air | Partly | Harbinger beats Blind Silver and Harpy; the Austringer has no Colonial counterpart; the amphibious Pegasus offsets part of it |
| The Pegasus is the only Colonial advantage | Not supported | It is a real advantage (amphibious), but infantry and surface ships also favour Colonials |
| War results show an imbalance | Not supported | 24 × 16 Wardens since 2023, but 4 × 2 Colonials since the LIVE merge |

## Imbalances worth raising with Siege Camp

1. **Ares vs Predator.** Same cost, and the Predator has +30% HP, +2,000 armour, +44% speed and better secondary weapons.
2. **Colonial fighters.** The Blind Silver costs the same as the Harbinger but has less HP and one weapon fewer. The Harpy costs more engines and parts than the Harbinger and loses on speed and damage per hit.
3. **Air strikes on ships.** Colonials have no dedicated answer to the Austringer.
4. **Anti-infantry weapons on top-tier tanks.** The Predator and Juggernaut have them; the Ares and Hasta don't.
5. **Poseidon hangar can't be reserved.** Any friendly player can withdraw the aircraft, which cancels out the capacity advantage in practice.
6. **Speed.** On battle tanks and super tanks, the Warden version is 15% to 44% faster.

## Doctrine differences (not imbalances)

- Armour variety: Wardens have scout, cruiser and tank destroyer lines; Colonials have six assault tanks, the Scorpion, the Ballista and tankettes.
- Submarines: Nakki hunter vs Trident endurance.
- Artillery: mobile Colonial vs emplaced, longer-range Warden.
