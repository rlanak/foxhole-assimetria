# War history

**Bottom line:** war results don't show a dominant faction. Since 2023 the Wardens have won 24 wars and the Colonials 16. Since the shards merged into LIVE (Update 1.64), it's 4 × 2 for the Colonials, with two stalemates.

| Period | Wardens | Colonials | Stalemates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wars 100–141 (Jan 2023 to Oct 2026) | 24 | 16 | 2 |
| Since the LIVE merge (Wars 135–141) | 2 | 4 | 1 |
| All wars in the game's history | 73 | 66 | — |

Full data in [`data/wars.csv`](../data/wars.csv).

## Things to keep in mind

- The Wardens won four wars in a row (Wars 130–133), spanning the Airborne launch (Feb 2026).
- Wars 138 and 139 lasted only 6 days each and carry little weight.
- War 142 started on 2026-10-27 and was ongoing at the last check.
- War results also measure population, coordination and time zones, not just equipment. If the asymmetry strongly favoured one side, recent results wouldn't be alternating.
- The wiki table doesn't cleanly separate the ABLE and CHARLIE shards before the merge. Wars 131–134 are labelled ABLE and later ones LIVE.
